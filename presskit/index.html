About the game

Tutsak is a co-op horror game for 1–4 players, inspired by games like Lethal Company and R.E.P.O. You and your friends search abandoned places for keys and try to escape before the creature catches you. Whoever gets caught is locked in a cell until the others break them out.

Every creature hunts in its own way, and there are lots of ways to betray your friends. There are also skinwalkers that imitate you.

Key features

  • Online co-op for 1–4 players
  • Every creature hunts in its own way
  • Lots of ways to betray your friends
  • Skinwalkers that imitate you
  • Proximity voice chat
  • Localized in 14 languages

About Roni Studio

Roni Studio is a one-person indie studio from Türkiye. Tutsak is made entirely by its solo developer, Roni.
