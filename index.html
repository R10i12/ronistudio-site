The game

Tutsak is a 1–4 player co-op horror game where you search for keys and escape before the creature catches you. Caught? You're locked in a cell until your friends break you out. Sometimes, you can sell out a teammate instead.

Online co-op · Proximity voice chat · 14 languages · PC (Steam)
